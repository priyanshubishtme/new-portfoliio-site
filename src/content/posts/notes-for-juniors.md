I'm only in my second year, so take this for what it is: notes from someone a single step ahead, not wisdom from the summit. But that step matters, because I remember exactly how confusing first year felt.

Start building before you feel ready. The gap between tutorials and real projects is where the actual learning lives. Pick something small — a to-do app, a calculator, a personal page — and finish it. "Finished" matters more than "perfect."

Show up to events even when you don't feel like you belong there. Hackathons, talks, club meetings — the people you meet there become the ones who pull you into opportunities later. And help the people behind you. Teaching what you just learned is the fastest way to make it stick.
