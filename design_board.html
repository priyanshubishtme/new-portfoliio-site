How it moves

The brief was soft, good animation. The rule is to spend motion in a few places that matter and keep the rest still.

  • The one big moment: the scroll sceneNight warms to dawn, the avatar rises, the campus pans, and the sun comes up. Slow and eased, never snappy.
  • The introA single star twinkles, then your name writes itself. It plays once and lasts about two seconds.
  • Project stackCards slide over each other as you scroll. This shows depth, so it earns its motion.
  • Small responsesLinks, buttons and the cursor glow answer the visitor's action. No fade-in on every section.
  • Reduced motionIf the visitor's device asks for less motion, the scene becomes a still, layered picture.
